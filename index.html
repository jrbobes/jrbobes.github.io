Space Shooter

Defend against incoming enemies! Destroy them all to advance to the next wave. Each enemy is worth 10 points!

Score: 0
Lives: 3
Wave: 1

How to Play

Controls: Arrow Keys or A/D to move left and right. Spacebar to shoot.

Objective: Destroy enemies before they reach the bottom!

Rules: Each enemy destroyed = 10 points. Difficulty increases each wave. Lose all 3 lives and it's game over!
